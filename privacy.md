# VocalLingo Privacy Policy

**Effective:** August 9, 2026

VocalLingo does not collect, transmit, store, or share any personal information.

The app runs entirely on your device. It does not require an account, does not connect to any server, does not use analytics, does not include advertising, and does not access your microphone, camera, contacts, location, or health data.

## How the app works

Audio playback uses Apple's built-in speech synthesizer (AVSpeechSynthesizer) to generate audio files that are cached locally on your device. These files are stored only in your app's private storage and are automatically removed when you uninstall VocalLingo.

## Data we collect

**None.** VocalLingo does not send any information off your device.

## Third-party services

VocalLingo does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising networks, crash reporters, or SDKs.

## Children's privacy

VocalLingo is rated 4+ and safe for all ages. Because we collect no data, we comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by design.

## Changes to this policy

If we make changes to this policy in a future version, we will update the "Effective" date above and note the changes here.
