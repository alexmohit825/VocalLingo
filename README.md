## Frequently asked questions

**Why does the app show "PREP 0/12" when I first open a unit?**
The app is pre-rendering audio for every phrase in the unit so playback stays smooth on CarPlay. This only happens the first time you open a unit. Later visits are instant.

**Does VocalLingo need an internet connection?**
No. All audio is generated on your device using Apple's system speech synthesizer. The app works fully offline.

**Does VocalLingo work with CarPlay?**
Yes. Connect your iPhone to your car (wired or wireless CarPlay). VocalLingo appears on the CarPlay dashboard as an audio app.

**Does VocalLingo collect any data?**
No. VocalLingo does not use analytics, does not include ads, and does not require an account. All progress is stored locally on your device.
